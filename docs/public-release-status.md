# K Scan AI Public Release Status

Last reviewed: August 20, 2026

## Purpose

This file is the internal source of truth for public website status language. Public marketing, support, beta,
download, and legal pages should not claim a feature is more mature than the status recorded here.

This document is internal. It is not a public route and should not be linked from any public page.

## Status Table

### Android
- Active public/beta access through Google Play as currently configured on the website.
- Build 29 is in final release/artifact/device certification.
- Do not imply every new Build 29 capability has completed physical certification merely because the source RC is
  frozen.

### iOS
- Active TestFlight access through the current app-specific TestFlight link.
- Build 29 source work is release-ready/frozen, with final physical/artifact certification still relevant.
- Do not call the app publicly released in the App Store until that is actually true.

### Scanner
- Core live product capability.
- Fashion-specific visual identification and commerce discovery are real.
- Do not publish unverified accuracy percentages or exact-match guarantees.

### Mirror Selfie
- Built as a real wardrobe-intake capability.
- Device/release quality certification remains relevant.
- Safe public language may describe support for mirror/selfie-based wardrobe intake where available.
- Do not publish the internal 10/10 staging result as a public accuracy claim.

### Closet
- Live product capability.
- Represents items the user identifies as owned.
- Keep distinct from Recent Scans, which represent discoveries.

### Saved Looks
- Built/current product capability where enabled in the current release.
- Do not imply features beyond the implemented Saved Looks experience.

### Elise text
- Live AI Stylist capability.
- Elise may operate with authorized fashion context.
- StyleChat is Elise's conversational capability, not a separate product.

### Elise spoken responses
- Real text-to-speech capability.
- Distinct from VoiceScan.
- May be described as optional spoken Elise responses where enabled.
- Do not imply microphone-based conversational voice input.

### Elise avatar / V10
- Engineering/integration exists.
- V10 remains in physical/shadow validation.
- Do not present V10 as a universally validated production avatar system yet.

### Dressing Rooms
- Live collaborative capability.
- Durable membership/sharing functionality exists.
- Report, block/unblock, share revocation and relevant backend blocking enforcement exist in the current Build 29
  status.
- Do not claim end-to-end encryption or zero-knowledge storage.

### Privacy Lens
- Native/on-device face-redaction architecture has been built for mobile.
- Fail-closed privacy architecture exists.
- Full production/device certification and universal activation must not be assumed.
- Safe wording: technology built / certification or rollout in progress.
- Unsafe wording: "Every image is automatically face-blurred before upload."
- Do not claim license-plate masking is complete.

### VoiceScan
- Coming Soon.
- Do not change VoiceScan to Live merely because Elise can speak.
- VoiceScan and Elise spoken responses are separate capabilities.

### Meta wearable experience
- Pre-production wearable experience.
- Browser/local prototype and connected-runtime work exist.
- Do not imply commercial availability on Ray-Ban Meta or other Meta hardware.
- Physical hardware certification remains separate.

### Android XR
- Pre-production Android XR / glasses program.
- Connected Runtime Phase A foundation has been completed according to the Build 29 status.
- Do not imply commercial availability or hardware certification.

### Apple Vision Pro
- Pre-production spatial program.
- Phase A Swift/SwiftUI visionOS work and browser simulation exist.
- Public website may describe the browser experience as a spatial-commerce simulation.
- Do not imply a commercially available Vision Pro app unless separately verified.

## General Claim Rules

- Public product brand: K Scan AI
- Tagline: See it. Scan it. Style it.
- Do not use public StyleDNA branding. Use Signature Style.
- Do not claim exact product matches.
- Do not guarantee prices, inventory, availability, sizing, authenticity, or retailer relationships.
- Do not claim universal face or license-plate masking.
- Do not claim wearable hardware availability.
- Do not claim K+ is active revenue.
- Do not claim a mature persistent cross-device Style DNA vault.
- Do not publish accuracy or performance percentages without an approved benchmark.
- Do not describe retailers as partners unless an actual partnership is verified.
